Jonna Health Sync

A personal, single-user tool

Jonna Health Sync is a private script that reads one person's own Fitbit data (sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, activity and weight) through the Google Health API once a day and stores it on that person's own computer for a personal training dashboard.

It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and serves exactly one user: the developer.
